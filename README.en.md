# Chinese Textbook Reading Index

A reading index organized by grade, volume and unit for students, families and learning-material organizers.
It retains 376 textbook entries and authors, without full literary texts, question banks or automatic explanations.

## Open the full catalog

Open [初高中目录.md](初高中目录.md), then use GitHub's document outline or browser search to find titles and authors.
No dependencies or service startup are required.

## Read by study stage

| Stage | Catalog coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Junior secondary | Grades seven, eight and nine, both volumes |
| Senior secondary compulsory | Upper and lower compulsory volumes |
| Senior secondary selective compulsory | Upper, middle and lower volumes |

The catalog is subdivided by unit and retains records for whole-book reading and activity units.
Use volume and unit alongside the title when identifying the teaching scope.

## Interpret exam labels

The labels “重点考点 / 考点 / 非考点” are inherited organizational annotations.
Textbook edition, region and examination sources are not fully specified; compare them with your textbook and teacher's requirements.
They are not current official examination standards or a substitute for a specific study assignment.

## Corrections and additions

Provide the title, author, volume, unit and textbook edition, with a verifiable source.
Explain the applicable edition when changing structure or labels, rather than mixing different textbook systems.

## Sources and permission

Listed works and authors retain their attribution; catalog maintenance does not acquire literary copyright.
